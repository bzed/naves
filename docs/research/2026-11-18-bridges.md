# Bridges by length, over roads, and their ground, as measured

Measured on 2026-11-18 on the Berchtesgaden example (`examples/berchtesgaden`, `terrain_size = 2048`) with
`cargo run --release -p naves-export --example road-analysis 2048` on the clean commit `03f76e7`, which holds a deck
to the junction at its end, reports `bridge_end_step` and cuts a shoulder ring in the end guard to the deck
(the figures of the last version of this note were measured at `8d54efe`; the diagnosis behind the change is the checkpoint
diagnosis of 2026-11-18). Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-bridges-and-terrain-design.md`.
All figures are at 2048; the baseline is d7cb864 (the commit before the bridge work), also at 2048. The run
took 759 s up to the end of the profiles (placement 755 s, conform 28.3 s). The per-bridge table of the run
(84 rows: OpenStreetMap length, part, growth at each end and the turn of the road it grew into, slide, ground above
the underside before and after the shaping, solid parts on a road below) is the `BRIDGETABLE` lines of the example's
output; the list of every finding with its place is the `RULES_OUT` file. Nothing was tuned for these figures.

Baseline: 65 built and 19 "no room". Now every bridge longer than 3 m is built from a part. Of the 14 deck ends
that grew past the bridge's end (to give a road below its opening or to meet the road), 12 grew into a road that turns
by at most 5 degrees. Two grew around a larger turn: 299428041 (7.2 degrees at its start, 1.3 m grown) and 36788104
(6.2 degrees, 2.4 m grown). Both are panel grids lengthened over a road below. The goal of "no deck grown around more
than 5 degrees" is **not met** by these two; they are reported, not changed.

The "64 spans" of the baseline were counted per span; the run reports ground per bridge. The example counts span parts
(202 on 79 bridges with a deck) but not the spans with ground above the underside, so the 64 has no counterpart in
this run.

- **Ground above the underside:** 33 bridges before the shaping, 6 after. The 6 are exactly the `bridge_buried`
  findings (the measure is the same): 48450084 0.81 m, 39537999 0.54 m, 258387240 0.52 m, 1341010238 0.27 m, 150368334
  0.19 m, 172178577 0.12 m. Before the deck was held from its end there were 20, 7 of them over 0.5 m (39674984 1.55 m,
  48450084 1.35 m, 130657216 1.29 m, 286196692 1.11 m, 74208396 0.84 m, 39537999 0.59 m, 258387240 0.52 m). The cause
  of 5 of the 20 was a **step at the joint**: the height solve did not tie a deck to the junction or road at its end,
  because a deck is one variable at its middle and every rule that holds a variable to a junction measured from there
  (a deck longer than about 10 m was not held at all). Now a deck is held from the end that faces the junction, a chain
  end at another chain's deck end takes the deck's height, and the shoulder ring of any road (the bridge's own included) inside the
  deck's end guard and width takes at most the deck's surface (13 of the 20 were such rings, 0.84 m or less).
- **The four game-check sites** (39674984 Bergwerkstrasse, 286196692, 48450084, 130657216): no `bridge_end_step` at any
  end (the step is under 0.5 m; the diagnosis' crude measure of the step at the joint was 0.00 m for all four after the
  fix, 1.48, 1.18, 0.64 and 1.29 m before), and 39674984, 286196692 and 130657216 are no longer buried. 48450084 is
  still buried by 0.81 m: a 10 m wide road (591438390, 11.8 % grade) leaves the end node at an acute angle and its core
  covers the deck's corner; that is geometry, not a step. The other residuals: 258387240 (two paths ending on each
  other 3.7 m from the port, not joined to the bridge), 39537999 and 150368334 (a node of bridges and a loop),
  1341010238 (the road below at deck level under the lengthened grid), 172178577 (the core of a through road).
- **Steps that remain (`bridge_end_step`, 3 after Task 8c; 4 before it):** a level deck cannot follow two junctions that want different heights,
  so where a deck has a junction at each end the second end keeps what is left, and it is reported now (no rule reported
  it before): 130047131 0.5 m above the junction at (2458, 6106) (-0.52 m) and 39663890 0.6 m below the junction at (2839,
  5196); and two where the road beyond a deck runs through a junction's patch off its plane (below). A deck end is compared with the junction that the height solve holds it to: one within the junction's
  patch, or, at the end of a chain, within 4 m beyond it, and nearer this end than the deck's other end. A first
  version compared further and reported 6: 240795460 at 2.6 m, 39605281 at 1.5 m, 74208396 at 1.1 m and 245085906
  at 0.5 m. The narrowed rule (run b) dropped four of them and left a real break unreported. Measured with the `ENDJOINT`
  lines (the deck, the strip joint beyond it, the junction with its patch and plane): in none of the four does the
  difference sit at the deck's own joint (the deck port is level with the joint beyond it to 0.6 m) or across a
  placement gap (the joints beyond are 13, 17, 13 and 13 m away: no gap). It sits **where the strip beyond the deck runs
  through the junction's patch**, held to nothing there. 240795460: the junction at (5701, 7091) is 6.2 m from the port
  (patch 5.0), the plane is 280.5 m and the strip 277.9 m: 2.4 m, a real break. 74208396: junction (6747, 7385), 5.7 m
  (patch 5.0), plane 320.8, strip 319.8: 0.8 m. 39605281: junction (5128, 7928), 9.5 m (patch 6.0), the strip is 0.44 m
  above the plane there: under the tolerance, the 1.5 m was the plane read at the port. 245085906: the junction is 3.3 m
  from the other end, which is level with it, so the 0.5 m was the plane extrapolated over the 6.6 m deck. So for a
  deck inside a chain, the strip's height where it passes closest to the junction inside the patch is compared with
  the plane there: a ramp between the deck and the patch (`road_grade`) and a plane extrapolated beyond the patch are
  not reported; 240795460 (2.4 m, seen in the game) and 74208396 (0.8 m) are reported again.
  **The cause is in the solve, and Task 8c fixes it for 240795460 and not for 74208396 (run at 2048: the first is +0.00 m, the second still +0.76 m; `bridge_end_step` 4 to 3, `road_conflict` 900 to 899 (the 2.5 m at (5700, 7088) is gone), `road_misses_terrain` 4286 to 4285, `road_not_drivable` unchanged at 185 (one at (5700.7, 7083.7) gone, one of 1 % over 4 m at (5599.3, 7126.6) new, the neighbouring heights moved by the solve), the 496713926, 130657216 and 40883071 lines unchanged):** a junction that names a road holds it only at a joint of the
  strip in its patch (or, `Arm`, the nearer variable of a free piece within 13 m). A strip that runs through the patch
  between a deck, which no hold takes (it is not free), and a joint that another junction holds has no variable there,
  so the junction holds nothing and the strip stays on the ground. Now such a junction holds the strip's interpolated
  height at the point nearest the junction inside the patch (a row between the two variables, `holds::crossings`,
  `model::add_crossing`); a junction in the patch of a deck's end is left to the deck's rules (a level deck is held to
  one junction; its other end stays a `bridge_end_step`). The row cannot always be kept: where keeping the plane costs more than the break, the solve gives the hold up (the row costs `HARD`, like a bend
  over its limit), and the break stays and is reported by `bridge_end_step`, which now
  looks at every junction of the bridge's roads within 25 m of the patch that the strip runs through (it looked only
  4 m beyond the patch). Only runs that touch a deck get the row; a plain road keeps `Arm`. The candidates 496713926,
  130657216 end 0 and 40883071 end 0 are **not** cases of this defect, which is why their lines are unchanged: in
  496713926 the joint beyond end 1 is 3.8 m from the junction, inside its patch (so `Patch` holds it), and the run beyond
  end 0 points away from it; in 40883071 the joint beyond end 0 is 2.9 m from the junction, inside its patch; in
  130657216 end 0 the run points away from the junction, which belongs to end 1, where the chain ends. With the wider reach the run at 2048 compares 496713926 end 1 at +0.30 m and 40883071 end 0 at -0.09 m (both under the 0.5 m tolerance); the other 21 new `ENDSTEP` lines are all within 0.31 m, so `bridge_end_step` stays at 3.
  At 74208396 the junction is also held by the deck 74208422, which lies on its plane, and the deck of 74208396 is
  level at the height of the junction at its other end. From the `ENDJOINT` line (port (6742.3, 7387.9), joint beyond
  (6758.0, 7380.2), 17.5 m; junction (6747, 7385)) the strip passes the junction at t = 0.31, 5.5 m from the port. The
  plane there is 320.58 m (strip 319.82 plus the 0.76 m step), or 320.81 m if read as `h_edge`: a hold needs the
  joint beyond at 319.71 + (0.87 to 1.10) / 0.31 = 322.5 to 323.3 m, 16 to 20 % over the 17.5 m run and 2.4 to 3.2 m
  above the joint's 320.07 now: a hump, not a ramp. The grade (16 to 20 %) is under the Asf2 ceiling (27 %); it is
  the change of grade at the deck's end that costs. The deck is level, so the bend there would be 0.16 to 0.20 against
  a limit of run / radius = 18.4 m / 400 m = 0.046 (run: midpoint of the 17.45 m segment and the 19.3 m deck); the bend
  rows cost `HARD * 12.5 m` per unit of excess linear plus the square, 1.4 to 1.9e4 and 2.0 to 3.7e4 for an excess of
  0.11 to 0.15, against 1.3e4 (0.76e4 + 0.58e4) for the 0.76 m of pin slack, and the hump needs a second bend where it
  comes down. This is derived from the code's weights and the run's numbers, the solve does not print its slacks. A
  heavier pin weight would force the hump; the right fix there is a strip joint at the patch (a variable), not a weight.
  A pitched deck would remove the steps and is not part of this change.
