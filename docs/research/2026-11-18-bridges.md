# Bridges by length, over roads, and their ground, as measured

Measured on 2026-11-18 on the Berchtesgaden example (`examples/berchtesgaden`, `terrain_size = 2048`) with
`cargo run --release -p naves-export --example road-analysis 2048` at commit `8d54efe` (plus the `JUDGED` and
`BRIDGETABLE` output that the example gained for this note). Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-bridges-and-terrain-design.md`.
All figures are at 2048; the baseline is d7cb864 (the commit before the bridge work), also at 2048. The run
took 770 s up to the end of the profiles (placement 767 s, conform 27.5 s). The per-bridge table of the run
(84 rows: OpenStreetMap length, part, growth at each end and the turn of the road it grew into, slide, ground above
the underside before and after the shaping, solid parts on a road below) is the `BRIDGETABLE` lines of the example's
output; the list of every finding with its place is the `RULES_OUT` file. Nothing was tuned for these figures.

## What the 84 OpenStreetMap bridges became

| Built from | Bridges |
|---|---|
| concrete panels (a grid of 1 or more, 128 of the 199 panels scaled) | 76 |
| `bridge_wood2` | 1 |
| `bridge_concrete3` | 1 |
| `bridge_metal_25` | 1 |
| plain road, at most 3 m (2.4 to 3.0 m), on purpose | 5 |
| plain road because the install has no part (`bridge_not_built`) | 0 |

Baseline: 65 built and 19 "no room". Now every bridge longer than 3 m is built from a part. Of the 14 deck ends
that grew past the bridge's end (to give a road below its opening or to meet the road), 12 grew into a road that turns
by at most 5 degrees. Two grew around a larger turn: 299428041 (7.2 degrees at its start, 1.3 m grown) and 36788104
(6.2 degrees, 2.4 m grown). Both are panel grids lengthened over a road below. The goal of "no deck grown around more
than 5 degrees" is **not met** by these two; they are reported, not changed.

The "64 spans" of the baseline were counted per span; the run counts bridges (the example prints both: 202 span parts
on 79 bridges with a deck). Read the two as the same order of measure, not the same unit.

- **Ground above the underside:** 32 bridges before the shaping, 20 after. The 20 are exactly the `bridge_buried`
  findings (the measure is the same). 7 of them are more than 0.5 m above the deck: 39674984 1.55 m, 48450084 1.35 m,
  130657216 1.29 m, 286196692 1.11 m, 74208396 0.84 m, 39537999 0.59 m, 258387240 0.52 m. The other 13 are 0.4 m
  or less.
- **Solid parts on a road below:** 8 before, 3 findings now (1.4 m of 21.0 m missing under 1341010238 Silbergstraße,
  0.4 m of 19.8 m under 74208396, 0.6 m of 12.8 m under 74208422).
- **Clearance:** 6 bridges lie over a road. Five were measured, all at 4.50 m of 4.5 m (before the height solve they
  were between -1.32 m and -0.26 m). The sixth (Silbergstraße) is not measured at a crossing point.
- **Road findings:** `road_misses_terrain` is 4292, the figure of the last run before this commit, and 25 below the baseline
  (4317); it is 21 above the figure before the ground work (4271). All other road counts equal the last run, and
  `road_not_drivable` and `road_grade` are at or below the baseline; `road_conflict` is 5 above it (905 against 900),
  as it was before the ground work. The findings file (`RULES_OUT`) of this run is byte-identical to the one of the
  last run on commit `21570c2`, before the fix of the fork's guard.
- **Deepest cut near a deck:** 12.4 m below the original ground. 7 bridges have ground under the deck lower than
  both the original ground and the ceiling by more than 0.1 m (a road below cuts there legitimately).

## Known open items

- **Bergwerkstrasse, way 39674984 (`bridge_metal_25`, 18.5 m):** the ground at the junction end of the deck is
  1.55 m above the deck's surface (3 vertices), held by the shoulder ring of 235908542. Decks are level, so where the
  ground at an end is higher than the deck the ring of the neighbouring road keeps it there. A user raises the
  bridge's road or moves the junction.
- **Silbergstrasse, way 1341010238 (a lengthened panel grid over a road, 8.8 m):** two approach pieces miss the
  terrain by 3.8 m (at 4692, 7337) and 2.8 m (at 4686, 7327), the bridge blocks the road below by 1.4 m of 21.0 m,
  and 0.14 m of ground (4 vertices) is above the underside.
- **Three `bridge_blocks_road` sites** (above): 1341010238, 74208396 and 74208422.
- **Turn over 5 degrees:** the two grown ends above.
- **Not tested in the game:** this note is about numbers from the analysis. The game check (the four bridges over roads,
  way 495868617, the worst buried spans, one bridge of each kind, a drive over each) is the controller's checkpoint
  before the figures go into the example's README.
