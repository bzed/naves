# Bridges by length, over roads, and their ground, as measured

Measured on 2026-11-18 on the Berchtesgaden example (`examples/berchtesgaden`, `terrain_size = 2048`) with
`cargo run --release -p naves-export --example road-analysis 2048` on commit `069517a` plus the change that holds a deck
to the junction at its end, reports `bridge_end_step` and cuts a shoulder ring in the end guard to the deck
(the figures of the last version of this note were measured at `8d54efe`; the diagnosis behind the change is the checkpoint
diagnosis of 2026-11-18). Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-bridges-and-terrain-design.md`.
All figures are at 2048; the baseline is d7cb864 (the commit before the bridge work), also at 2048. The run
took 770 s up to the end of the profiles (placement 767 s, conform 27.5 s). The per-bridge table of the run
(84 rows: OpenStreetMap length, part, growth at each end and the turn of the road it grew into, slide, ground above
the underside before and after the shaping, solid parts on a road below) is the `BRIDGETABLE` lines of the example's
output; the list of every finding with its place is the `RULES_OUT` file. Nothing was tuned for these figures.

| plain road, at most 3 m (2.4 to 3.0 m), on purpose | 5 |

Baseline: 65 built and 19 "no room". Now every bridge longer than 3 m is built from a part. Of the 14 deck ends
that grew past the bridge's end (to give a road below its opening or to meet the road), 12 grew into a road that turns
by at most 5 degrees. Two grew around a larger turn: 299428041 (7.2 degrees at its start, 1.3 m grown) and 36788104
(6.2 degrees, 2.4 m grown). Both are panel grids lengthened over a road below. The goal of "no deck grown around more
than 5 degrees" is **not met** by these two; they are reported, not changed.

The "64 spans" of the baseline were counted per span; the run reports ground per bridge. The example counts span parts
(202 on 79 bridges with a deck) but not the spans with ground above the underside, so the 64 has no counterpart in
this run.

- **Ground above the underside:** 33 bridges before the shaping, 6 after. The 6 are exactly the `bridge_buried`
  findings (the measure is the same): 48450084 0.81 m, 39537999 0.54 m, 258387240 0.52 m, 1341010238 0.27 m, 150368334
  0.19 m, 172178577 0.12 m. Before the deck was held from its end there were 20, 7 of them over 0.5 m (39674984 1.55 m,
  48450084 1.35 m, 130657216 1.29 m, 286196692 1.11 m, 74208396 0.84 m, 39537999 0.59 m, 258387240 0.52 m). The cause
  of 5 of the 20 was a **step at the joint**: the height solve did not tie a deck to the junction or road at its end,
  because a deck is one variable at its middle and every rule that holds a variable to a junction measured from there
  (a deck longer than about 10 m was not held at all). Now a deck is held from the end that faces the junction, a chain
  end at another chain's deck end takes the deck's height, and the shoulder ring of any road (the bridge's own included) inside the
  deck's end guard and width takes at most the deck's surface (13 of the 20 were such rings, 0.84 m or less).
- **The four game-check sites** (39674984 Bergwerkstrasse, 286196692, 48450084, 130657216): no `bridge_end_step` at any
  end (the step is under 0.5 m; the diagnosis' crude measure of the step at the joint was 0.00 m for all four after the
  fix, 1.48, 1.18, 0.64 and 1.29 m before), and 39674984, 286196692 and 130657216 are no longer buried. 48450084 is
  still buried by 0.81 m: a 10 m wide road (591438390, 11.8 % grade) leaves the end node at an acute angle and its core
  covers the deck's corner; that is geometry, not a step. The other residuals: 258387240 (two paths ending on each
  other 3.7 m from the port, not joined to the bridge), 39537999 and 150368334 (a node of bridges and a loop),
  1341010238 (the road below at deck level under the lengthened grid), 172178577 (the core of a through road).
- **Steps that remain (`bridge_end_step`, 6):** a level deck cannot follow two junctions that want different heights,
  so where a deck has a junction at each end the second end keeps what is left, and it is reported now (no rule
  reported it before). 240795460 2.6 m below the junction at (5701, 7091), 39605281 1.5 m above the junction at (5128,
  7928), 74208396 1.1 m below at (6747, 7385), 39663890 0.6 m below, 130047131 and 245085906 0.5 m above (just over the
  0.5 m tolerance). A pitched deck would remove them and is not part of this change.
- **Solid parts on a road below:** 8 before, 3 findings now (1.4 m of 21.0 m missing under 1341010238 Silbergstraße,
  0.4 m of 19.8 m under 74208396, 0.6 m of 12.8 m under 74208422).
- **Worse than before:** `bridge_clearance` is 1 and `road_grade` is 4. Both are 299428041 (2349, 6546): the road below
  (98335907) starts at the bridge's own end node, 2 m from the deck's port, and loops under the deck. Before the deck was
  held it floated at 415.4 m and the road started at 409.7 m, a 5.7 m step that no rule reported. Now the road starts at
  the deck's height and cannot get under it within 13 m: 2.3 m of the 4.5 m of clearance and a crest of 35 m vertical
  radius (60 m is the minimum). Neither answer is drivable; it is a placement or data problem that is now reported
  honestly.
- **Clearance:** 6 bridges lie over a road. Five were measured: four at 4.50 m of 4.5 m and 299428041 at 2.30 m (above); without the clearance in the
  height solve they were between -1.32 m and -0.26 m. The sixth, Silbergstraße (1341010238), is one of the four
  bridges over roads that the spec judges; it reached 4.50 m at the baseline and is **not measured** now (the OpenStreetMap
  crossing is not where the placed lower road runs under the lengthened deck). The goal "the four bridges over roads
  keep their clearance" is therefore shown for three of the four and not shown for Silbergstraße; the game check has
  to show it.
- **Road findings:** `road_misses_terrain` is 4286: 31 below the baseline (4317), but **15 worse than before the ground
  work (4271)**, which is reported as worse and was not tuned (4292 before the deck was held from its end).
  `road_conflict` is 900 (905 before; the baseline is 900), `road_not_drivable` 185 (186; baseline 189), `road_gap` 356
  and `road_kink` 21 are unchanged; `road_grade` is one worse (above).
- **Deepest cut near a deck:** 12.4 m below the original ground, at 28885465 (`bridge_wood2`) at (6088, 6620). 7 bridges
  have ground under the deck lower than both the original ground and the ceiling by more than 0.1 m: 6 are bridges over
  roads (a road below cuts there); the seventh, 23458060 at (5684, 5760), is 0.10 m.

## Known open items

- **Bergwerkstrasse, way 39674984 (`bridge_metal_25`, 18.5 m):** was 1.55 m of ground above the deck at the junction
  end. The deck sat 1.48 m below the junction (a 25 m deck ends 12.5 m from its middle, out of the End rule's 10 m); it
  is now held to the junction and is not buried.
- **Pitched decks:** not done. The six `bridge_end_step` findings are where a level deck has junctions at both ends that
  want different heights; a deck with a pitch needs a pitched object in the game, which has not been checked.
- **Silbergstrasse, way 1341010238 (a lengthened panel grid over a road, 8.8 m):** two approach pieces miss the
  terrain by 3.8 m (at 4692, 7337) and 2.8 m (at 4686, 7327), the bridge blocks the road below by 1.4 m of 21.0 m,
  and 0.14 m of ground (4 vertices) is above the underside. Its clearance over the road below is not measured (see
  above).
- **Three `bridge_blocks_road` sites** (above): 1341010238, 74208396 and 74208422.
- **Turn over 5 degrees:** the two grown ends above.
- **Not tested in the game:** this note is about numbers from the analysis. The game check (the four bridges over roads,
  way 495868617, the worst buried spans, one bridge of each kind, a drive over each) is the controller's checkpoint
  before the figures go into the example's README.
